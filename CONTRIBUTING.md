# Contributing to RAWDOG

Author: Nicholas Corrieri  
GitHub: @nickcorrieri  
Owner: InnoNotion LLC

RAWDOG is local-first, append-only archival tooling for photographers. Keep
changes conservative, explicit, and safe for user-controlled photo archives.

## Contribution License

By submitting a pull request, patch, issue attachment, code sample, design,
documentation change, or other contribution to RAWDOG, you agree that your
contribution is submitted under the Apache License, Version 2.0.

Unless explicitly stated otherwise in writing, contributions use the same
license as the project. Do not submit code or assets that you do not have the
right to contribute.

## Developer Certificate

By contributing, you certify that:

- you wrote the contribution or have the right to submit it;
- the contribution may be licensed under Apache License 2.0;
- the contribution does not knowingly include secrets, private credentials,
  private user data, or proprietary third-party material;
- you understand the contribution may be modified, redistributed, or removed.

## Safety Standard

Contributions must preserve RAWDOG's core safety rules:

- no archive deletion;
- no archive overwrite;
- no original-file renaming;
- no silent project assignment;
- no sync semantics;
- no automatic collision resolution.
